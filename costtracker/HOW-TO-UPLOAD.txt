CostTracker test build – how to put it online

1. Open github.com/steffiwallace/cost-calendar
2. Click "Add file" > "Upload files"
3. Drag the whole "costtracker" FOLDER (not just the files inside it) onto the page
4. Click "Commit changes"
5. Wait 1–2 minutes for GitHub Pages to update (the Actions tab shows a green tick when it's done)
6. Open on your phone:  https://steffiwallace.github.io/cost-calendar/costtracker/

Your current app at https://steffiwallace.github.io/cost-calendar/ is not changed.

Good to know
- This test version starts fresh each time you open it. Nothing you add is saved yet.
- Face ID, Apple Pay and bank connection are simulated, as in the prototype.
- To add it to your home screen: open the link in Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen.
- Main.dc.html and canvas.json in the repo don't do anything on the website. You can delete them or leave them.
